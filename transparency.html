Ethical Standards

Our Commitments

We aim to establish transparent processes, maintain appropriate records, and communicate honestly about our progress.

Responsible Use of Donations

We aim to allocate donations in accordance with the relevant campaign terms, agreed funding conditions, and our published donation policy.

Clear Program Information

We aim to communicate our program objectives, activities, and outcomes accurately, using verified information.

Financial Records

We aim to maintain appropriate financial records and make relevant financial information available in accordance with our legal obligations, organizational policies, and reporting capacity.

Governance & Organization

Verified information about our legal structure, registration status, governance, and responsible representatives will be published when available and appropriate.

Safeguarding & Ethical Conduct

We aim to promote respectful treatment of beneficiaries, responsible handling of personal information, and appropriate safeguarding practices across our activities.

Feedback & Complaints

We aim to provide a clear channel through which donors, partners, volunteers, and community members can raise questions, concerns, or complaints.
