Policy Document 01

Privacy Policy

MeoCare respects the privacy of individuals who visit our website, contact our team, make donations, express interest in volunteering, or submit partnership inquiries.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when individuals interact with our website.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the services used, we may collect information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, country of residence, organization details, messages, donation records, and information voluntarily provided through forms.

Payment Information Protocol:

Payment information, where applicable, should be processed through an appropriately selected payment provider. MeoCare should not collect or store full payment card details directly unless it has the necessary compliant systems and authorization.

2. How We May Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to inquiries and requests.
  • Coordinate donations and provide relevant confirmations.
  • Communicate about programs and approved activities.
  • Assess partnership and volunteer inquiries.
  • Maintain appropriate administrative and financial records.
  • Protect website security and prevent misuse.
  • Meet applicable legal and reporting obligations.

3. Sharing of Information

Personal information should be shared only when necessary for legitimate organizational purposes, with appropriate service providers, or where required or permitted by applicable law.

We should not sell personal information to third parties.

4. Data Security and Retention

MeoCare aims to use appropriate safeguards to protect personal information and retain it only for as long as necessary for legitimate purposes or applicable legal obligations.

5. Your Choices and Requests

Individuals may contact MeoCare to ask questions about their information or make a privacy-related request, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Policy Document 02

Donation and Fund Allocation Policy

MeoCare is committed to the highest standards of financial stewardship, integrity, and transparency. This policy sets out the principles governing how contributions are received, accounted for, and utilized.

1. Allocation of Contributions

Donations will be allocated in accordance with the campaign terms selected by the supporter (e.g. Food Assistance & Humanitarian Aid or Animal Rescue & Welfare), agreed funding agreements, and published fund policies. General contributions support the broader mission where most critically required.

2. Processing & Receipts

Donation methods, international payment options, receipts, and any applicable tax treatment will be provided only after the relevant banking arrangements and regulatory requirements have been verified.

3. Resource Stewardship

We aim to use available resources carefully and focus on practical, meaningful outcomes. Regular progress updates and verified financial summaries will be made accessible as reporting capacity expands.

Policy Document 03

Safeguarding Policy

MeoCare places paramount importance on the protection, dignity, and rights of every individual and animal touched by our programs.

1. Beneficiary Dignity & Consent

Assistance is provided without discrimination. Beneficiaries are treated with respect, kindness, and honor. Personal narratives and imagery are captured and published only with documented, informed consent.

2. Animal Welfare Standards

All animal rescue, feeding, and medical interventions are conducted humanely in collaboration with licensed veterinary clinics and certified animal handlers to prevent distress, neglect, or harm.

3. Staff and Volunteer Conduct

Volunteers and team members undergo orientation regarding ethical interactions, safeguarding obligations, and privacy compliance.
