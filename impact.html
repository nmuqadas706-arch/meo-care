Dignity, Consent & Privacy Safeguards

MeoCare respects the dignity of all beneficiaries. Personal stories and photographs are documented strictly with informed written consent and adhere to ethical safeguarding standards. Verified archives are available in our News & Stories section.

Accurate Activity Records

Financial Traceability

Maintain appropriate records of donations and expenditure according to transparent guidelines.
